# Parallel units: what `--jobs 4` bought

Raw numbers: [2026-10-16-parallel.json](2026-10-16-parallel.json). Design: [docs/parallel.md](../../docs/parallel.md). Claude Haiku 5.5, `--output patch`, the hand plans of `eval/services/` at `--max-tokens 48000` with the service guide on main, `build/cancho-agent` and `project` built from this branch; `eval/run_service_plan.py ... --jobs 1|4`, runs alternated (jobs 1, jobs 4) on one Mac. **Two runs per cell is noise-prone**; read the direction and the reasons, not the second digit.

## Services (project driver, wall-clock of the whole run)

| service | run | jobs 1 | jobs 4 | speedup | agent time (sum of turns) 1 / 4 | critical path 1 / 4 | cost 1 / 4 | attack 1 / 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| webhooks | 1 | 171.5 s | 131.7 s | 1.30x | 140 / 142 s | 111 / 116 s | $0.0219 / $0.0232 | 129 / 129 |
| webhooks | 2 | 168.9 s | 149.4 s | 1.13x | 138 / 168 s | 115 / 136 s | $0.0228 / $0.0257 | 129 / 129 |
| kvstore | 1 | 246.2 s | 203.6 s | 1.21x | 196 / 220 s | 151 / 172 s | $0.0283 / $0.0326 | 224 / 225 |
| kvstore | 2 | 262.9 s | 194.9 s | 1.35x | 239 / 216 s | 192 / 170 s | $0.0356 / $0.0315 | 224 / 224 |

Mean speedup 1.25x (1.13x to 1.35x). Calls 14-15 (webhooks) and 12-14 (kvstore) in every run; output tokens 32-39k and 46-56k; the cost differences are the model's run-to-run variation (jobs 1 alone varies kvstore by $0.007), not a cost of parallelism. The attack score did not fall (kvstore 225/225 once at jobs 4, 224/225 otherwise: the same single failure as the jobs 1 runs). **Integration requeues: 0 in all eight runs. Rate-limit waits: 0** (four units at once did not meet a 429 on this key).

Critical path = the longest chain of the plan weighed by each unit's agent time (the journal's `ms`); "agent time" = the sum of all turns. The ratio agent time / critical path is the most any number of jobs could give by overlapping agent turns alone: 1.2x to 1.3x for these runs.

## Why not more

- **The last unit is half the run.** The application unit (the whole `app_handle` with thinking) took 93-155 s of agent time in a 132-263 s run and nothing can run beside it: it depends on every other unit. The pure units together cost 34-47 s of agent time in webhooks (4-9 s for the longest) and 75-85 s in kvstore (30-39 s for the longest, one slow unit that bounds the parallel part).
- **What is left is checking, one thing at a time.** After the application verifies, the sweep of all pure units' tests (about 25 s for 14 units) and the final gate (about 17 s) run in the driver, as they do at jobs 1. Parallel units do not make those shorter.
- **The first version was slower (206 s at jobs 1, 242 s at jobs 4 for webhooks run 1).** Pure units cost a second or two of model time and several seconds of checking; at every merge the driver re-ran every verified unit's tests, one at a time, so with 14 integrations the driver was the bottleneck and units waited in its queue (`bearer_span`: 36 s from dispatch to end, 4 s of it the agent). The fix is a rule, not a speed-up of the checks: a unit that only ADDED items to the file (`splice.rewrites` = 0) cannot have changed what the verified units do (a clashing name is refused by the merge, a file that does not compile by the combined check), so only a unit that rewrote something makes them run again. The final gate still runs everything once. That change is what turned 0.85x into 1.25x.
- The sums of agent time are a little longer at jobs 4 in most runs (4 calls in flight against one key): the provider's latency under concurrency, not extra work.

## A library (ini, planner's plan: layers of 3, 1, 2, 2, 2, 2 units)

| | unit phase (first unit start to last unit end) | agent time | critical path |
|---|---|---|---|
| jobs 1 | 173.0 s | 154 s | 126 s |
| jobs 4 | 175.7 s | 171 s | 145 s |

**No speedup (0.98x).** The plan is a chain of six layers; at most three units are ready at once, only at the start, and the model's turns on this plan are 1-6 s each against 3-10 s of checking per unit. This is the bound measured before this change (libraries at most 1.37x overall, 718 s of agent time against a 524 s critical path over 13 hand plans) showing up: parallel units help plans that are wide AND whose units are slow to write; library plans are neither. (Cost and run seconds of the jobs 1 row include the planner; the jobs 4 run reused its `plan.json` through `run_build.py --plan`, so only the unit phases are compared. Both audits scored 0/76, the same, an audit problem of this item unrelated to the driver.)

## What this does not show

- Two runs per cell, one machine, one key, one model. A rate limit was never reached, so the 429 handling is tested only against a stub (`scripts/test-parallel.sh`).
- Webhooks and kvstore only; jobs and invoices have layers `[5,2,1]` and `[6,2,1]` and would behave like webhooks.
- A plan that is wide with slow units (many equally big functions) is where `--jobs` should pay; none of the plans measured is like that.
